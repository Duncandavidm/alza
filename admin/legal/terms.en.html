1. What MonAvi is

MonAvi is a personal finance app: it helps you record transactions, accounts, credit cards, bills to pay, debts, budgets and savings goals, prioritize your payments, and earn cashback for your verified electronic invoices, coupons for your good financial habits, and discounts and vouchers at partner merchants. MonAvi is not a bank or a wallet: it does not hold your money, does not move real money between accounts, does not connect to your bank accounts, and does not replace professional financial, accounting or legal advice.

3. Rules for using the app

This is how MonAvi works and what we expect from everyone who uses it:

Recording your money

  • Log your income, expenses, transfers, savings and payments manually, by voice, by scanning an invoice, or with Apple Shortcuts. Each transaction is saved to the account you choose and changes its balance within the app (not at your bank).
  • Transactions the app detects (an Apple Pay payment or a notification from your bank) are never saved automatically: they appear as “To confirm,” and you decide whether to record, edit or discard them.
  • Payment reminders (“Did you pay…?”) keep appearing until you answer whether you paid or not. The “x” only hides them for now.
  • Editing and deleting: swipe a transaction right to edit it or left to delete it (the app asks you to confirm before deleting). When you delete it, its amount returns to the account balance.
  • Transactions with an invoice: a transaction that comes from a read invoice (scanned, shared as a PDF or photo, or attached to an expense via “Did they give you a receipt?”) keeps the invoice amount and date: these cannot be changed, and the amount adjusts automatically to the value confirmed by the DGI (Panama's tax authority, Dirección General de Ingresos). You can change its category and description. If you delete it, the cashback for that invoice is removed (if the invoice was split by category and you delete only one part, the proportional part is removed) and the invoice is voided: it cannot be registered again.
  • Record only your own finances, or those of your household with their permission. Do not record third parties' data without their consent.

Invoices

  • Scan the invoice's QR code with the camera, choose a photo, or share it as a PDF with MonAvi.
  • Only register invoices for purchases you made. Do not scan other people's invoices, invoices collected from other people, or invoices from a merchant.
  • Each invoice can be registered only once across all of MonAvi. The date and cashback rules are in section 5.

Benefits at merchants

  • Redemption, discount and voucher codes are personal: show them at the checkout from your phone. Do not sell them, post them or share them.
  • Follow the conditions of each benefit (validity period, minimum purchase, uses per person and branches).

What is not allowed

  • Using MonAvi for illegal activities or to launder money.
  • Registering invoices that are fake, altered or not yours, creating duplicate or fake accounts, or inviting yourself.
  • Modifying the app, automating its use with programs (bots), copying its content, or attempting to breach its security or that of partner merchants.
  • Reselling, transferring or exchanging cashback, coupons or benefits for money.
  • Harassing or deceiving the staff of partner merchants.

If you do not follow these rules, we may cancel the cashback, coupons and benefits obtained that way, and suspend or close the account (see section 14).

5. Invoices and cashback

  • How you earn it: when you register an electronic invoice issued in Panama, MonAvi verifies it with the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI). When the DGI confirms it, you receive cashback of up to 1% of the total paid (for example, $0.84 for an $84.25 invoice registered the same day). The invoice is verified when you register it; if the DGI has not yet published it, it is checked again every 24 hours for up to 7 days, and the cashback remains pending until it is confirmed.
  • Which invoices can be registered and how much cashback they earn (based on the days between the invoice date and the day you register it, in Panama time):
    When you register itIs it registered?Cashback
    In the same month, up to 3 days after the purchaseYesFull (1%)
    In the same month, 4 to 7 days afterYesHalf
    In the same month, 8 or more days afterYesOne quarter
    Invoice from the previous month, registered on the 1st through the 3rd of the monthYes, with the invoice dateNo cashback
    Invoice from the previous month after the 3rd, or olderNo—
  • Income backing: cashback is credited when the income you record in MonAvi backs your purchases. The invoice and the expense are always recorded, but if you have no recorded income backing the purchase, the cashback remains pending. An income entry counts as backing 48 hours after you record it in the app; therefore, if you record your income after scanning the invoice, the pending cashback is released 48 hours after you record it. Cashback that is not backed by income within 45 days expires.
  • Cap on unverified income and verification: to back cashback, recorded income counts up to US$3,000 per month (in Colombia, COP 12,000,000) while it is not verified. You can record as much income as you like for your budget; the cap only applies to cashback. If you earn more, you can verify your income by uploading a document from the app (pay stub or proof of payment, employment letter, certificate from the Caja de Seguro Social (Social Security Fund), bank statement or income tax return). Once approved, the approved amount counts for 6 months. MonAvi may also ask you to verify your income when it detects inconsistent information.
  • Cashback review: if we detect unusual patterns (for example, income that does not match your history, income recorded just to release cashback, income edited after releasing it, many invoices from different merchants in a short time, or several accounts on the same device, with the same phone number or with the same Apple subscription), your new cashback may remain pending while we review it. If the review confirms false information, other people's invoices or duplicate accounts, we may cancel the cashback obtained that way. Only one account per person is allowed.
  • One subscription, one account: the Pro subscription on your Apple ID activates the benefits on only one MonAvi account at a time. If you restore it on another account, Pro moves to that account, the previous one loses it, and the new account's cashback may be placed under review.
  • Each invoice can be registered only once and by only one account. Invoices that the DGI does not confirm within 7 days do not earn cashback. The amount that counts is the one confirmed by the DGI.
  • There is a cashback cap per person per day. Percentages and caps may change; changes do not affect cashback you have already earned.
  • It is not cash: cashback can only be used at the partner merchants shown in the app (to redeem products or unlock discounts and vouchers). It cannot be withdrawn, transferred to another person, or exchanged for money.
  • If we detect fraud or abuse (for example, invoices that are not yours, duplicate accounts or manipulation of the app), we may cancel the cashback obtained that way and suspend the account.
  • If you delete your account, any cashback you have not used is lost.

6. Good habit coupons

  • How you earn them: once a day, MonAvi checks on its servers whether you met the rules of the current coupons: for example, saving a percentage of your income in the month, reaching a savings goal on time, staying within all your budgets for the month, logging your transactions on many days of the month or every day of the week, or having good financial health. The app shows in “Good habit coupons” which coupons are available, at which merchant, and what you need to do.
  • When you meet a rule, the coupon arrives automatically in “My redemptions” as a gift, with its code and its expiration date, and we let you know with a notification. It is given once per rule per period, and some coupons are limited in number.
  • Days are counted by the date on which you log, not by the date you assign to the transaction, and savings must add up with the income and expenses you recorded.
  • Coupons are not money: they cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for money or for cashback. If a coupon expires unused, it is lost.
  • If we detect false records or manipulation to earn coupons, we may cancel them and suspend the account.

7. Benefits and redemptions at partner merchants

  • Products with cashback: when you redeem a product, its value is deducted from your cashback and you receive a 6-character code that you show at the merchant. The merchant validates it and gives you the product at no cost. The code expires after 7 days; if you do not use it, the value is automatically returned to your cashback.
  • Discounts and vouchers unlocked with cashback: some partner merchants offer a discount (for example, 20%) or a voucher (for example, $20) that is unlocked with the cashback indicated in the app. The app shows you how much more you need to unlock it. When you unlock it, that cashback is deducted and the benefit becomes available to use at the checkout within the number of days shown in the app, under the conditions stated (for example, a minimum purchase). When you use it at the checkout, a code is generated that lasts 15 minutes. If you do not use it in time, the cashback is returned. The discount is applied by the merchant; a voucher may be paid for by the merchant or by MonAvi, at no additional cost to you.
  • Free discounts and vouchers for MonAvi users: some merchants offer benefits that do not require cashback. They are used by showing the code at the checkout, subject to the limit of uses per person and per period shown in the app.
  • Coupons and gifts (for good habits or sent by MonAvi) expire on the date shown in the app and, if not used, are lost. We notify you before they expire.
  • No benefit is money: it cannot be exchanged for cash, cannot be transferred, and may be subject to limited availability, dates and branches. The available benefits may change at any time; those you have already unlocked are honored until their expiration.
  • You can add your valid codes to Apple Wallet. The pass is only a copy of the code: the one that counts is the one in the app, and a code that has already been used or has expired stops working even if it is still in Wallet.
  • Each partner merchant is responsible for its product, its availability and its customer service. If you have a problem with a redemption, write to us and we will resolve it with the merchant.

  • A campaign reward is added to your cashback, under the same rules as sections 5 and 7.
