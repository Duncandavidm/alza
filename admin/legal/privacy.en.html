MonAvi ("we," "the app") is a personal finance app developed and operated by David Muñoz Duncan, a natural person, RUC 8-NT-2-793713 DV 95, in the Republic of Panama, who is the data controller responsible for processing your personal data under Law 81 of 2019 (Panama's personal data protection law) (contact: hola@maday.app). This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for, who we share it with, and what control you have over it.

In short: we collect your account data and what you record in the app so that it works, usage and error data to maintain and improve it, and—only if you agree—we use your data for notices about promotions and for anonymous studies. We do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not sell your personal data, we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not collect your location or your contacts. Photos, audio, and notifications from your bank are processed on your iPhone and are not uploaded.

2. What data we collect, in detail

  • Income verification (optional): if you choose to verify your income for cashback, the document you upload (pay stub, employment letter, Caja de Seguro Social (CSS) certificate, account statement, or income tax return). It is stored in private storage, only the MonAvi team sees it and, if you allowed the use of AI, our AI provider (Anthropic) reads it solely to suggest your name and your monthly income. The document is deleted when the review is complete: we keep only the approved amount, the date, and the document type.
  • Fraud prevention: a random identifier for your device, created by the app and stored in the iPhone Keychain, to detect multiple accounts on the same phone. It is not the advertising identifier, it does not identify you outside MonAvi, and it is not shared with third parties. We also use Apple's DeviceCheck: Apple stores two yes/no flags per iPhone (whether it has already had a MonAvi account and whether an account on that iPhone had cashback voided). Apple does not receive your name, your email, or any other data about you, and you do not see any notice or permission request for this.
  • Account: your email address or, if you sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple provides to us (if you choose to hide your email, we never see it).
  • Financial data you enter: accounts, transactions, budgets, bills to pay, debts, and credit cards (credit limit, interest rate, minimum payment, and payment day), savings goals, invoices, and any other data you enter in the app. MonAvi does not connect to your bank or read your bank accounts: you record everything yourself (manually, by voice, by scanning an invoice, or with an Apple Shortcut that you set up).
  • Invoices you scan (optional): if you use "Receipts that count," the unique code (CUFE) of your electronic invoices and the invoice date and, when the DGI (Panama's tax authority) confirms them, the data on that invoice: the merchant that issued it, the date and time of purchase, the amount, the payment method (for example, "credit card" or "cash," never your card number), and the itemized list of products purchased. With that detail we show you "Your purchases" and the monthly summary of your invoices. We also store when you recorded each invoice, because cashback depends on the number of days that have passed since the purchase (see the Terms of Use).
  • Invoices you share with us as a PDF or photo: if you open an invoice with "Share → MonAvi" or choose a photo, the file is read on your device only to find its QR code or its CUFE, and is then deleted. We do not upload or store the file; only the invoice code, just as if you had scanned it with the camera.
  • Cashback, benefits, and redemptions: the cashback you earn for your invoices, the coupons you earn for your good habits (we calculate them on our servers based on the transactions, budgets, and savings goals you record), the discounts and bonuses you unlock with your cashback, the free discounts you use, and the products you redeem (which benefit, at which merchant, the code, when it expires, and whether it has already been used).
  • Invitations: if you invite someone with your code or join with another person's code, we store who invited whom in order to give them the invitation cashback.
  • Profile (optional): your name, phone number, age, gender, and other data you choose to give us in the initial survey (marital status, dependents, occupation, goals). You can leave them blank or change them in Settings > My profile.
  • Permissions you give: if you agreed to the use of artificial intelligence, to receiving promotions, or to the anonymous studies, we store your answer and the date, in order to respect it.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, the identifier of your device that Apple provides to us to send you notifications (for example, "You earned cashback," "Discount unlocked," or "Your coupon expires tomorrow"). It is deleted when you sign out.
  • Subscription: the status of your subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple; we only receive the confirmation.
  • App usage: the date of your entries, invoices, and redemptions, the notifications you open, and how many times you use the artificial intelligence features (to apply the fair-use limits).
  • Diagnostics and errors: daily reports from Apple's MetricKit (crashes, unexpected shutdowns, performance, app and iOS version). They do not include your transactions.
  • Support and suggestions: what you write to us by email or from the app.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Notifications from your bank (optional): if you set up an automation in the Shortcuts app that passes MonAvi the text of a notification, an SMS, or an email from your bank or wallet, that text is read on your device only to find the amount, the merchant or the person who sent you the money, and the last 4 digits of the card. The text is not uploaded or stored; security codes, declined purchases, and promotions are ignored. The transaction remains pending confirmation on your phone and is saved to your account only when you record it. To suggest the account to you, your iPhone remembers which account you used with those 4 digits.
  • Apple Pay payments (optional): if you set up the "Transaction" automation in the Shortcuts app, MonAvi receives the amount and merchant that Shortcuts provides and shows you "Payment detected" so that you can record it. MonAvi does not have access to Apple Pay or your Wallet.
  • Widgets: the "Your day" widget shows on your Home Screen what you spent today and this month, what you have left, and your latest transactions. That data is stored on your iPhone for the widget (you can hide the amounts with the eye icon) and is deleted when you sign out or delete the app. The Lock Screen widgets and the Control Center button only open MonAvi.
  • Apple Wallet: if you add a coupon to Apple Wallet, the pass is generated by our server with the code, the merchant, the expiration date, and the location of the merchant's store (not yours). It is your iPhone, without sending us anything, that decides to show you the pass when you are near that store.

3. What we use your data for

  • To show you your financial information and make the app work.
  • To generate the artificial intelligence tips, summaries, and answers you request in the app (for example, "How much did I spend on food this month?" or what to pay first).
  • To verify your invoices with the DGI, calculate and credit your cashback, give you coupons for good habits, and manage your benefits, redemptions, and discounts.
  • To send you the reminders and notifications you turn on: due dates and payments (scheduled by your own device) and notifications such as "You earned cashback," "Discount unlocked," or "Your coupon expires tomorrow" (sent by our server through Apple).
  • To verify your subscription and give you access to paid features.
  • To find and fix errors and improve the app (diagnostics and usage data).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse (for example, duplicate invoices or fake accounts).
  • Only if you agree: to prepare market studies with anonymous and aggregated data, as explained in section 6.
  • To apply brand campaigns ("buy X and earn more"): we review the details of your verified invoices to determine whether you bought the campaign product and give you the reward.
  • Only if you agree ("Get promotions and gifts"): to send you notifications of offers from merchants and brands, gifts such as cashback, coupons, or free products, and reminders to scan invoices again. To choose who receives each notification, we may use your age, gender, how much you use the app, when you scanned your last invoice, and what you bought according to your invoices. We never give your data to the merchant or the brand. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy.
  • To respond to your support messages and suggestions.

We never sell your personal data or use it to show you third-party advertising.

4. Speech recognition

If you record a transaction by voice, the audio is converted to text by iOS speech recognition (on your device when supported). Only the text is sent to our server to interpret the transaction. MonAvi never stores or transmits audio recordings.

5. Who we share data with

Only with the providers we need to operate the app:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and server functions.
  • Anthropic — processes the financial information needed to answer what you ask the artificial intelligence (insights, questions, payment advice, interpretation of a dictated transaction). It is sent only if you give us permission in the app (you can withdraw it in Settings > Privacy). Anthropic does not use it to train its models, according to its commercial API policy.
  • Brevo — sends the app's emails (for example, the code to confirm your email or reset your password). It receives only your email address and the content of that message.
  • Vercel — hosts our web pages (this policy, the support page, and the redemption page used by partner merchants).
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, diagnostic reports (MetricKit), and delivery of notifications and Apple Wallet passes (if you choose to add a coupon).
  • Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of Panama — we look up the CUFE of the invoices you scan on its public website to confirm that they are genuine. We do not send it your personal data.
  • Brands with campaigns — they receive only campaign totals (number of verified sales, units, and amount, by week and by merchant, with at least 5 people). They never receive your name, your email, or your invoices.
  • The partner merchant where you use a benefit — only if you have a redemption, an unlocked benefit, or a pending gift at that merchant, the checkout can see your first name with your last initial, the last 4 digits of your phone number, the code, and the benefit, in order to find you and apply it. It never sees your full email address, your balance, or your transactions.
  • Authorities — only if a law or a court order requires us to.

6. Market studies with anonymous data

You participate only if you agree. The app asks you on a separate screen ("Your purchases, anonymous") and we store your answer with the date. You can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy; if you do not agree, you still earn cashback and coupons.

Using the information from verified invoices and from the transactions recorded in MonAvi, we prepare aggregated statistics on consumer habits: for example, the most used payment methods, the most purchased categories, brands, and products, the times, days, and dates of the month with the most purchases, the average ticket, purchase frequency, or how these figures are distributed by age range and gender. We may share or sell these reports to partner merchants, brands, and supermarkets, and use them to improve MonAvi.

  • The reports never include data that identifies you: not your name, your email, or your individual transactions. They show only totals, averages, and percentages for groups of people.
  • No group of fewer than 5 people appears in a report, so that the purchase of a particular person cannot be inferred.
  • If a filter leaves a group of fewer than 5 people, the report does not show any detail.
  • If you stop participating, your data is excluded from studies generated from that point on. You can also request this at hola@maday.app.

7. Security

Your session is stored encrypted in your device's Keychain. Each user can read and modify only their own data: this is enforced in the database (Row Level Security), not only in the app. All connections are encrypted. You can protect the app with Face ID, Touch ID, or your iPhone passcode in Settings > Security. More details in Data Protection.

8. Retention of your data

We keep your data for as long as you have your account. Diagnostic reports are used only to fix errors. If you delete your account, your data is deleted immediately from our database, except for information that a law requires us to keep and anonymous studies already prepared (which do not identify you). Encrypted backups from our database provider are overwritten periodically.

International transfer: our servers and providers are located in the United States. By using MonAvi, your data is transferred there, with the security measures described in this policy; see Law 81 of 2019.

9. Your rights

You can ask us at any time, by writing to hola@maday.app, for:

  • Access: a copy of the data we hold about you (you can also export your transactions to CSV from Settings).
  • Rectification: the correction of inaccurate data.
  • Cancellation: the complete deletion of your account and your data (you can also do this yourself in Settings > Delete account).
  • Objection: that your data not be used for promotions or included in the anonymous market studies.
  • Portability: your transactions in a format you can take to another service (CSV).
  • Withdrawal of the permissions you gave (artificial intelligence, promotions, studies) in Settings > Privacy.

We respond within the time limits established by Panama's Law 81 of 2019 on Personal Data Protection and its implementing regulations. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Autoridad Nacional de Transparencia y Acceso a la Información (ANTAI). More details in Law 81 of 2019.
